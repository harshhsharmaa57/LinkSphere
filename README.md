# 🌐 LinkSphere

**Discover, Save, and Share the Best Resources on the Internet.**

LinkSphere is a community-driven platform where people come together to share the most useful links they've found across the web — from learning resources and developer tools to career opportunities and hidden gems of the internet.

Think of it as **Pinterest meets Product Hunt, but for links.**

---

## ✨ What You Can Do

### 🔗 Share What You Find
Found an amazing tutorial, a life-changing tool, or a website nobody knows about? Submit it to LinkSphere with a title, description, and category so others can discover it too.

### 🏠 Browse a Curated Feed
Explore a public feed of links shared by the community. Filter by category — whether you're into AI/ML, web development, cybersecurity, or just looking for entertainment — there's something for everyone.

### ⬆️ Vote on the Best Stuff
Upvote the links you love and downvote the ones that don't deliver. The best content rises to the top.

### 🔖 Save for Later
Found something useful but don't have time right now? Save it to your personal collection and come back to it whenever you want.

### 📂 Organize into Collections
Create themed boards — like "Frontend Inspiration", "Interview Prep", or "Weekend Reads" — and add your favorite links to them. Share your collections publicly so others can benefit too.

### 👤 Build Your Profile
Every user gets a public profile showcasing the links they've shared and the collections they've built. It's your corner of the internet, curated by you.

### 🔍 Search Everything
Looking for something specific? Search across all links by title, description, or tags to find exactly what you need.
